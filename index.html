My Journey

Looking back, many of my decisions seemed "risky" because they went against the grain. But they were consistent in one thing: I kept choosing depth, long-term value, and technical foundations over short-term comfort. I don't regret the detours. They compounded.

2022.06
Decision: Chose a young autonomous driving startup (PhiGent) over a big-tech internship that most of my peers pursued.
Story: I chose hands-on learning and faster feedback loops. Lost the glitz of big-tech, but I got to physically test and experience dozens of different autonomous vehicles, transforming from someone who barely knew cars into a total car enthusiast. 🚗✨
2023.03
Decision: Chose a Zeekr PnC algorithm internship instead of a more predictable product-manager path at Horizon Robotics.
Story: I leaned into depth over comfort, diving into first principles by developing a C/C++ based ADAS system—a truly massive industrial-scale framework 💻. I'll never forget the scorching summer in Jiading, Shanghai, the boiling hot seats of the Zeekr 001 test cars 🥵, and my amazing, supportive colleagues. Hearing the TLA feature I worked on mentioned at the official product launch made my heart race. I proudly earned an SP-level return offer. Huge thanks to the Zeekr ADAS team, the place where my engineering skills were truly forged! Production deployment is the ultimate engineering crucible! 🔥🚗🛠️
2023.12
Decision: Chose to continue research at CASIA instead of a SenseTime autonomous driving internship offer.
Story: Engineering helped me grow💪, but research fascinates me✨. I chose rigor and long-horizon questions—the kind of work that compounds, even if it looks slower at first. A massive thank you to the professors and peers at the CASIA DRL Lab—this was the birthplace of my research enlightenment. 🌱📚
2024.06
Decision: Chose to pursue a PhD instead of lucrative SP offers from Huawei Vehicle BU and Zeekr.
Story: I chose long-term growth and independence, betting on compounding skills over early stability. Deeply grateful for my family's unwavering support❤️. RIP to all the LeetCode problems I grinded🙏, though TuT. I guess I won't be "Trapping Rain Water" with dynamic programming anytime soon 😢🌧️.
2024.09
Decision: Chose embodied AI—joined PsiBot and Peking University as a research intern.
Story: I followed genuine curiosity and impact, moving toward scalable robot learning for open-world real life. Here, I learned how to drive a research project independently from start to finish and how to elegantly bridge industry and academia—this really feels like the best setup I could have asked for. 🤖✨
2026
Goal: Hope to do something meaningful in embodied AI while keeping my hairline intact. 💻🤡
Story: Beyond the papers, I hope to keep that same "heart-racing" excitement I felt in the Zeekr test cars. Whether the loss curve goes down or the robot arm goes haywire, as long as I'm still solving hard problems with cool people, it's a win. (And please—stop staying up so late!) ✌️
