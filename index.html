Kefei Zhu

"I enjoy doing research with real-world impact, and I hope one day robots can step out of the lab and become true companions in our daily lives."

I am a Research Scientist at PsiBot, working on robot foundation models. My research interests include in-context learning in robotics, world models, RL post-training, and learning dexterous manipulation from human data.

Before joining PsiBot full-time in 2026, I conducted my PhD research at the Peking University–PsiBot Joint Lab, advised by Prof. Yaodong Yang and Yuanpei Chen, and worked closely with Prof. Hao Dong.

If you are interested in collaborating, feel free to reach out by email.
